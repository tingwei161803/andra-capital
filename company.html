The Firm

What Andra Capital is, how its funds are structured, and how the marketing numbers compare with its regulatory filings.

Andra Capital is a growth- and late-stage technology fund founded in 2016 and headquartered at 750 Battery Street, San Francisco. It targets pre-IPO companies in AI, data, cloud, cybersecurity and fintech, and pitches a shorter exit horizon than traditional venture capital.
